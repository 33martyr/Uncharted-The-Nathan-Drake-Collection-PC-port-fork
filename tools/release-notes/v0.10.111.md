**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer: maps

Every multiplayer and co-op map was compared with the PS3 version and the map converter now follows the PS3 1.19
maps much more closely. The fixes are general rules, so they apply to every map and to the lobby backgrounds.

- **Skies:** the flat red-brown sky (Airstrip) and the skies made of colourful blocks are gone; skies are converted
  in the right format with the matching sky shader.
- **Moving textures:** textures that slid when they should not stand still again, and water, caustics, waterfalls and
  drips move the way they do on PS3.
- **Water and waterfalls:** water surfaces use the game's own water layout and reflections, the Fort co-op ocean is
  drawn as water again, and waterfall foam blends like on PS3. Still open: the Sanctuary waterfall and Museum's pink
  sea.
- **London:** many invisible walls are gone, and the graffiti and shutter decals are drawn on the walls instead of as
  floating opaque shapes.
- **Airstrip:** the doubled control tower and the textures where they did not belong are gone; the map now loads
  its world level like PS3 does.
- **London Underground:** after the train you no longer fall out of the map; the station is loaded again.
- **No more white flames, lamp glows or railings (Sanctuary, Shambhala):** particle effects and materials take their draw mode
  from the PS3 data.
- **See-through surfaces:** walls full of holes and surfaces you could look through are solid again; cut-out and
  see-through parts follow the PS3 settings.
- **Better material choices:** gates, doors, statues, marble, trims, boats, cars, cranes and handrails get a PS4
  material of their own kind instead of an unrelated one (Ruins' gates were drawn like a rug).
- **Hunter shirts** and other character clothes show their own colours.
- **PS3 1.19 effects:** particle effects that only the PS3 1.19 version has are now converted and added (8 effect
  files, for example Ruins and Fort).
- **Brightness:** maps that ask for the PS3 "improved" tonemap curve (Museum, Ruins, parts of Warzone, Borneo co-op)
  now get it, so Museum is less washed out. Set `UNCHARTED_IMPROVED_TONEMAP=0` to go back to the previous look. Still
  open: Village and some other maps are still too bright.
- **Boosters:** the booster lists, their order, slots and tiers are now the ones the online PS3 1.19 game uses.
- The world-level and collision fix for London Underground started from a pull request by 33martyr (PR 16). Thank you!
- **The update rebuilds the Uncharted 3 multiplayer maps** (the converters changed), which takes a while once. Your
  progress is kept.
