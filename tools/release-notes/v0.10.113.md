**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Museum: no more pink sea.** Geometry that only exists for collision (the sea bed under Museum's ocean and a few
  small pieces on other maps) is no longer drawn.
- **Missing map sounds are back:** rain, waterfalls, sea, fires and room ambience that only the PS3 1.19 version has
  now play on the multiplayer and co-op maps (Cave, Fort, Gompa, Graveyard, London, Oasis, Ruins, Sanctuary,
  Temple, Train Wreck, Village, Warzone, Yemen, Airstrip, Borneo and more).
- **Sound effects no longer garbled:** the sound banks keep their sounds exactly as the PS3 version has them instead
  of re-encoding them. The import no longer needs FFmpeg for the sound banks.
- **Multiplayer music restored:** the match win / lose and game-over stingers, level-up stingers, the multiplayer
  remixes, Power Play and the co-op endings were silent; they are now copied from your PS3 disc.
- **Your emblem returns after a match:** back in the lobby after a match, your emblem was empty. Multiplayer helper
  v7.214 asks for it again.
- **Brightness:** the PS3 improved tonemap curve from 0.10.111 is off again by default (Borneo co-op looks as in 0.10.110).
- After the update the launcher rebuilds the changed parts of your Uncharted 3 multiplayer import (some maps, the sound
  banks and the new sound steps) and the multiplayer image once; your progress is kept.

## Uncharted 2 multiplayer

- The Uncharted 2 import uses the same sound bank converter, so its sounds are fixed the same way. After the update
  the launcher converts the Uncharted 2 multiplayer files again once (about a minute or two).
