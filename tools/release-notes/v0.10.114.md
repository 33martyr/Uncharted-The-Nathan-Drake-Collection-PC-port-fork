**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer: maps and look

- **Brightness:** Museum, Ruins, Warzone and Shambhala use PS3's improved tonemap curve (Museum no longer washed out);
  Village fixed; Borneo co-op unchanged.
- **Texture tiling fixed on 33 maps:** textures that were stretched or repeated at the wrong size (for example the
  Village floor) now tile as on PS3.
- **Emblems on map walls:** the player emblems are drawn on the walls and boards of all 17 multiplayer maps.
- **Lobby banner emblem:** your emblem shows on the lobby banner again.
- **Fort:** the sea looks like water again. **Graveyard and Fort:** each kind of water uses the matching water surface.
- **Cave:** no more white panels, and the blurry pillar is sharp.
- **Surfaces seen from the wrong side:** one-sided surfaces no longer disappear or show through.
- **Borneo trees** look as on PS3.
- **Glass** is see-through again, and the white patches on the ice in Shambhala and Facility are gone.
- **Shiny and reflective surfaces (roofs, brass, marble) keep their shine (Museum and other maps).**
- **Cars and props** use their own PS3 textures at full detail.
- **Desert Village:** the climbing spots and thatch roofs are back as before, thatch drawn from both sides.

## Sound

- **Sound import update fix:** imports that already had the old sound banks did not get the 0.10.113 sound fix on
  update. The banks are now converted again.

After the update the launcher rebuilds the changed parts of your Uncharted 3 multiplayer import (most maps, some
characters and props, the sound banks) and the multiplayer image (helper v7.217) once; your progress is kept.
